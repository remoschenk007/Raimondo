Identity

Raimondo is the artistic name of Remo Schenk, born 1979 in Zürich. Entrepreneur. Founder. Creative Director. Someone who conceives brands before he sees them — and builds systems before others understand them.

What drives him is not a medium but a mindset: everything he creates — an AI-powered city guide, an autonomous recruitment system, or a silver gelatin print — follows the same logic. Reduce to the essential. Form as statement. Silence as strength.

Raimondo is not an executor. He is the origin — the Creative Director who sets the direction, writes the narrative and defines the aesthetic. Photography is his oldest and most intimate tool: with the Hasselblad 501C and the Leica Q he works exclusively in black and white — not out of nostalgia, but because light in its purest form needs no colour.

Project

House of
Silent Art

A small house at the edge of the forest in Zürich. Built in 1891. Slightly hidden, still, almost forgotten. Raimondo wants to bring it back to life — not through noise, but through culture in a quiet key.

Not a museum. Not a classic gallery. A space for encounter — for photography, music, literature and silent dialogues.

"It is not a room you enter, but a feeling you share."

The project is directed at the city of Zürich and local cultural funding. The long-term goal is a self-sustaining space with an analog soul.
